Now the most important concept, CTC means cost to company. It is the total amount the company spends on you in a year. Not the amount that reaches your bank account. CTC includes things you do not receive every month. The company's own contribution to your provident fund. Gratuity which you only get if you complete 5 years and in some offers a variable or performance pay that is paid later and is not guaranteed. On top of that, some money is deducted from your monthly salary. Your own provident fund contribution, professional tax in many states and income tax if your income is high enough. Let's break down a 4 lakh package, step by step. This is an example. Every company structures it a little differently. Say your basic salary is 40% of CTC which is 1 lakh 60,000 a year. The company's PF contribution is 12% of basic. That is 19,200 a year. Gratuity is set aside at about 4.81% of basic roughly 7,700 a year. Take those out and your gross salary is about 3 lakh 73,000 a year or about 31,100 a month. Now the monthly deductions. Your own PF is also 12% of basic which is 1,600 a month. Professional tax is about 200 rupees a month in many states and at this income under the new tax regime your income tax is zero. So your monthly enhanced salary comes to about 29,300 rupees. Not 33,000. Now back to the pattern from earlier. Why does a Gen C next or a digital role pay much more than the regular role for the same fresher? Because the company expects you to be productive on harder work sooner, better coding, faster problem solving and sometimes specialist skills. And the selection for those tracks mostly comes down to one thing, your performance in the coding round and the technical interview. So if you want a higher band, focus on coding at rice, strings, hashing, basic dynamic programming and SQL. That preparation can be the difference between three and a half lakh and six and a half lakh from day one. To sum up your CTC is the company's total cost, not your monthly salary. A four lakh package usually means around 29,000 in hand depending on the structure. And the fastest way to a bigger package as a fresher is the higher coding track.

## Clean script (what the viewer will hear)

Your job offer can be taken back after you have already signed it, and the reason is often one small mismatch that you never noticed. This video is for every fresher who has an offer and is waiting to join. By the end, you will know exactly what background verification checks, the three mistakes that cause trouble, and what to do this week so that nothing blocks your joining. First, what is background verification? Companies call it "BGV" after you accept the offer, the company hands your details to a verification agency. That agency checks that you are who you say you are, and that what you wrote in your forms is true. Usually, four things are checked. One, your identity, like your name, date of birth, and address. Two, your education, meaning your degree, your marks, and your college. Three, your past work, if you have any internships or earlier jobs. And four, in many companies, a criminal record check based on your address. Here is the part most students miss. The agency does not judge you, it only compares what is written in your application with what your documents and your college say. So, your job is simple, make everything match. Mistake number one is the one that catches the most people. Your name is written differently in different places. Maybe your certificate says one spelling, your adhar card says another, and you typed a third in the form. Maybe your date of birth is different on your 10th certificate and on your other documents. To you, it looks small. To the agency, it is a mismatch, and a mismatch becomes a male asking you to explain. So, today put your 10th certificate, your degree documents, your adhar, and your PIN card side by side. Check every spelling and every date. If something is wrong, do not wait for the male. Start the correction now, because corrections take time, and time is exactly what you do not have after you get the joining date. Mistake number two is hiding something that is easy to find. A backlog you did not mention, a year gap, or a degree that is still not complete. Here is a story, a fresher, let us call him Ravi, wrote that he had no backlogs. The college records showed one subject was still pending. The offer did not stop because of the backlog itself. It stopped because he had written something that was not true. This is the rule that matters. A backlog or a gap can often be explained. A false statement is much harder to explain. Companies know that real life is not perfect. What they do not accept is being told something untrue. So, if you have a pending subject, a gap year, or a change of course, write it honestly and keep a short clear reason ready. Mistake number three is silence. The agency sends you a mail or a link asking you to upload documents or confirm details. Many freshers miss it because it lands in spam or promotions or because the sender is a name they have never heard of. When you do not reply, your file stays open. And an open file can delay your joining letter even when your whole batch is moving. So, do three things, check your spam and promotions folders every few days. Look for mails mentioning verification, documents or an insufficiency. And reply within a day or two with clear scans, not blurry phone photos. One more thing, a real agency will never ask you to pay money for verification. If someone asks you to pay, treat it as a fake. Now, let us make this practical. Here is your checklist for this week. Keep a folder with scans of your 10th and 12th certificates, all semester mark sheets, your degree or provisional certificate, your ADHAR and PAN cards, your offer letter and any internship letters. Name the files clearly so that you can send them in minutes. Next, write down your own details exactly as they appear on your documents and use those same words every time you fill a form. And last, save the contact of your college exam office because the agency may call them to confirm your marks. If you do these three things, a mismatch will not surprise you and your joining will not be stuck because of paperwork. So, to sum up, verification only compares your forms with the truth, make your names and dates match, be honest about backlogs and gaps and never ignore the verification mail.

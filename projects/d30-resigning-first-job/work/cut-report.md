## Clean script (what the viewer will hear)

Quitting your first job the wrong way can follow you into your next one, and the damage often starts on your last day. Most freshers do not find out until the new company asks for one particular letter. This video is for every fresher who is thinking of switching now or after a year. By the end, you will know the letter you must not leave without, the clause to read before you resign, and the mistake that quietly delays your last salary. Before you write a single word of resignation, open your offer letter and appointment letter, two things matter. The notice period and any clause about training cost or a service bond. Notice period means the time you must keep working after you resign. It is whatever your own contract says, and it differs from company to company. Some say 30 days, some say 60 or 90. Do not copy a friend's number, read yours. Now, the clause most freshers miss, some contracts say that if you leave early, you repay training cost. A fresher has even reported a company asking for 90,000 rupees after he completed his notice period. That is one report, not a rule, but it shows why you read first. Here is the second step, resign in writing by email to your manager and to HR together. A phone call or a chat message is not enough because you need a date you can prove. Keep the email short and polite. Say you are resigning, give the date, and ask what your last working day will be under your notice period. Do not write anger, do not name the new company, and do not criticize anyone. Then save a copy of the email and the reply. This is your proof if anything goes wrong later. One more tip, do this only after your new offer is safely in your hand and your joining date is confirmed. Resigning first on a promise is a risk no fresher should take. Now, the letter I promised, it is called the relieving letter. It is the company's confirmation that you resigned, finished your notice and handed over properly. Your next employer or its background verification team may ask for it. Without it, a new offer can be held or even cancelled. We covered how background verification works in an earlier video. An experienced letter is a different paper. It describes your role and the time you worked. Ask for both. Some companies give them on the last day and some after a few weeks. So, ask HR in advance how long it takes and note the date. If the letter is delayed, do not shout. Send a polite reminder by email with your last working day and your clearance proof. A calm written trail works far better than an angry phone call. Before your last day, return the laptop, the access card and any other company items. Get the clearance done and ask for it in writing. Your final payment is usually settled after that. It is often called the full and final settlement. It can include your last salary and any leave pay your contact allows. Ask HR when it will arrive and check it against your salary slips. Also download your salary slips, your form 16 details and your provident fund details before your email access ends. After your last day, those links may stop working. Let me recap one. Read your notice period and bond clause before you resign. Two, resign in writing and keep the replies.

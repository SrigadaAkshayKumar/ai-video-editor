## Clean script (what the viewer will hear)

To freshers get the same question. Why should we hire you? One says, "Sir, I am hardworking," and a quick learner. The other gets the offer. The difference is not marks. It is one sentence structure that most students never learn. If you are a fresher preparing for an HR round, stay with me. By the end, you will know a simple three-part answer, the two lines that sound weak to every interviewer, and a trap in the last 10 seconds that spoils good answers. First understand why they ask this. The interviewer is not asking you to praise yourself. They have many candidates with the same degree and similar marks. This question is a polite way of asking three things. Can you do the work? Will you fit in the team? And did you actually prepare for this company? Now, think about the most common answers. I am hardworking. I am a quick learner. I am a team player. Every single candidate says these. When 50 students say the same line, the line carries no information. So, your job is simple. Replace claims with proof. Now, the three parts. Part 1. Pick one skill that the job needs and prove it with a small real example. Do not list five skills. Pick one. If the role is a developer role, it can be problem solving or building small projects. Then give evidence in one or two sentences. For example, you can say I like solving problems step by step. In my final year, I built a small attendance app and when it kept crashing, I found the bug by testing one screen at a time. See the difference? The word hardworking is a claim. The attendance app is proof. The interviewer can ask you about it and that is exactly what you want. But here is the catch. Proof only works if it is true. If you say something you cannot explain, the next question will finish you. Part 2. Connect your skill to what this company actually does. Before any interview, spend 10 minutes on the company website. Read what services they offer, what kind of clients they serve, and what the job description asks for. Then add one sentence. This role needs careful testing and clear communication and that is the way I already work. This one sentence tells the interviewer that you did your homework. Most students do not, so you stand out immediately. Now a warning. Do not copy lines from the company website word for word. Interviewers have read their own website many times. Say it in your own simple words with your own example. Do not compare yourself with other candidates either. Never say I am better than others. You do not know the others. Talk only about yourself. Part 3. Show that you will keep learning because a fresher is hired for potential. Nobody expects a fresher to know everything. So close with one line about growth. For example, I am still new but I learn fast when I get feedback and in my project I improved the code three times after my guide's comments. Now the trap I promised you. It is in the last 10 seconds. Many students finish a good answer and then add but sir, I have no experience so please give me a chance. That one line undoes everything. You just told them you are a risk. And with confidence instead, stop after your growth line, smile and wait. Silence after a good answer is not awkward. It is calm. So here is what to do this week. Write your answer in 4 or 5 sentences. One skill, one real example. One line about their work, one line about learning. Then say it out loud and keep it under 60 seconds. Record it on your phone and listen once. You will hear every week line yourself. Practice with a friend too and ask them one thing. Did it sound like me or like a script? Quick recap.

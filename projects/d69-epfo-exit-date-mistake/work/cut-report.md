## Clean script (what the viewer will hear)

One small date on a government website can stop your new company from clearing your background check, and most freshers never look at it until it is too late. If you plan to switch jobs after your first year, this video is for you. By the end, you will know which date it is, where to check it, and how to fix it yourself without begging your old company. Stay till the end, because the fix has a two-month rule that most people miss. The date is called the Date of Exit. It is the day your service ended with your old company, as recorded in your Provident Fund Account. When you leave a job, your employer is supposed to enter this date on the EPFO portal. EPFO is the employee's Provident Fund Organisation, the body that runs your PF. If your employer forgets or enters the wrong day, your record shows either no exit at all, or an exit that does not match your relieving letter. Why should you care? Because of two things. First, EPFO needs the date of exit of the old job to let you transfer your old PF online. Second, your new company's background check can see the mismatch. Here is what it looks like in real life. Imagine Rabi leaves his first company on the 31st of March, his old employer never marks the exit. In April, he joins a new company. During background verification, the old record still shows him working at the previous company. Now the new company sees two jobs at the same time. That is called overlapping dates. Mind reported in July that several salaried people who were between jobs were facing trouble joining a new company because of exactly these overlapping exit dates. So the problem is not that you did anything wrong. The problem is that a record you cannot see was left open, and you will not know unless you check. So here is step 1. Check it. It takes 5 minutes. Log in to the official EPFO member portal with your UAN and password. Open the service history section for every old employer. Look at the date of exit. Does it match your reliving letter and your last salary slip? If you have just finished a job, give it a little time. But if the date is missing or wrong, move to the next step. And one warning, use only the official member portal. EPFO itself wants members not to share their UAN password. Adhar, PAN or bank details with anyone who calls, claiming to be from EPFO. Step 2. The fix. You do not always need your old employer. EPFO lets eligible members mark their own exit. Go to the manage tab and click mark exit. First check that no date is already marked. Then choose the reason for leaving. Enter the correct date and complete the Adhar OTP verification. Now the rules and this is the part most people miss. At least 2 months must have passed since you left the job. The date you enter must fall within the month of your last PF contribution. And your UAN must be active and linked to Adhar with that mobile number in your hand. If your old employer entered a wrong date, do not just type a new one. Check your salary slips and reliving letter first. Then use the joint declaration option under the manage tab. Your employer approves it online and then the EPFO office updates the record. So here is your plan. Before you resign, save your appointment letter, salary slips and letter your reliving letter. When you leave, ask your employer to mark the exit date in the PF system. 2 months later, check your service history. If it is wrong, fix it before your new background check starts not after. The trap is waiting. If you wait until the new company sends an insufficiency mail, you lose days and sometimes the joining date. If the portal does not let you correct it and your old employer does not reply, you can file a complaint through the EPFO grievance system. Quick recap. Check the date of exit, match it with your reliving letter, fix it yourself after 2 months and use joint declaration if a wrong date is already there.

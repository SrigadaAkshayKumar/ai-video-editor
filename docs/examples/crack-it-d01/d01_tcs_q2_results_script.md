**Filled with the actual Q2 FY27 results (announced Oct 8, 2026).** Figures from news coverage of the results (Business Standard, BusinessToday, Zee Business): revenue ₹73,188 cr (~11% YoY; +1.3% QoQ, +0.5% QoQ in constant currency) · operating margin 24% · net profit ₹13,884 cr per most outlets (one outlet reports ₹13,934 cr, so the voiceover says "about ₹13,900 crore") · deals (TCV) $9.6 bn · annualised AI revenue $3.1 bn (>10% of revenue) · headcount 598,056 (+4,258, third straight quarterly rise) · attrition 13.3% (from 13.6%). Fresher context: CEO K Krithivasan said in April 2026 that TCS made 25,000 fresher offers for FY27 (versus 40,000+ a year earlier) and more hiring depends on demand.

**Verify before recording**
- Revenue, growth, operating margin, net profit: official press release.

- Any management comment on fresher or campus hiring: earnings call or press-conference reports.

### S02 · The headline numbers in plain words

```vo
First, the headline numbers, quickly.

T.C.S. reported revenue of about seventy three thousand two hundred crore rupees, which is a growth of about eleven percent compared to the same quarter last year. But in constant currency, which removes the effect of exchange rates, revenue grew only about half a percent compared to the previous quarter.

The operating margin came in at twenty four percent, and net profit was about thirteen thousand nine hundred crore rupees, up around fifteen percent from last year. T.C.S. also signed new deals worth nine point six billion U.S. dollars during the quarter.

So what does that mean in simple terms? It was a steady quarter. Profit grew well, and deal wins were healthy, but the underlying business growth was slow.

But remember, a company can grow its profit and still hire fewer people. That is why the next three numbers matter more.
```

### S03 · Number one: headcount

```vo
Number one, headcount. This is the total number of employees at the end of the quarter.

T.C.S. ended the quarter with five lakh ninety eight thousand and fifty six employees. That is a net addition of four thousand two hundred and fifty eight people compared to the previous quarter. And this is the third quarter in a row where T.C.S. has added people.

When headcount goes up, it usually means the company is bringing people in faster than people are leaving. Three quarters of growth in a row is a positive sign for pending joiners.

Keep in mind, headcount includes everyone, from freshers to senior employees. So it gives the direction, not the exact fresher picture.
```

### S04 · Number two: attrition

```vo
Number two, attrition. That is the percentage of employees leaving the company over the last twelve months.

T.C.S. reported attrition of thirteen point three percent, slightly lower than thirteen point six percent last quarter.

Why does this matter to you? When people leave, companies need to fill those seats, and freshers are often the cheapest and most scalable way to do that. So higher attrition can create room for new joiners. This quarter, attrition dipped a little, but headcount still went up. That means T.C.S. is adding people even though fewer are leaving, which is a good combination for freshers.
```

### S05 · Number three: what TCS said about hiring

```vo
Number three, and the most important one. What has T.C.S. said about hiring freshers?

Earlier this year, T.C.S. C.E.O. K. Krithivasan said the company had made twenty five thousand fresher offers for this financial year. That is much lower than the more than forty thousand freshers T.C.S. hired in each of the previous few years. He also said that hiring more would depend on how client demand improves.

In this quarter's results, the big message was A.I. T.C.S. said its yearly A.I. revenue has reached about three point one billion U.S. dollars, which is now more than ten percent of its total revenue. And T.C.S. has also been talking about building a large team of forward deployed A.I. engineers, who work directly with clients. I will explain exactly what that role is in tomorrow's video.

Here is how to read this. Companies rarely give a fresher number every quarter. So what you are looking for is the tone. Are they talking about hiring, or only about efficiency and A.I.? This quarter, the tone was cautious, but improving. Fewer fresher offers than before, but headcount is growing again.
```
